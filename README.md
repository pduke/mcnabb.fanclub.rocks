# The McNabb Family Archive

**Family History and Biographies, 1702–2025**

A digital collection of McNabb–McNeil family history, genealogical research, photographs, biographies, personal recollections, and historical documents.

**[Visit the Family Archive](https://pduke.github.io/mcnabb.fanclub.rocks/)**

## Origins of the Archive

The historical foundation of this collection comes primarily from the genealogical research of **Walter Craig Davidson**, who spent many years investigating the McNabb, McNeil, and related family lines.

According to family recollections passed down by Beverly Nan McNabb Orr, Craig's research included trips to Scotland to investigate historical records.

Warren Neil McNabb obtained a copy of Craig's research and shared it with his sister Nan and their cousin Jimmy Nagle. They continued expanding the family history, adding more recent generations and additional information.

Nan eventually assembled her research into notebooks and distributed copies to her children. Her son Paul Duke used one of those notebooks as the foundation for the family website.

## The Digital Archive

Developed and maintained by **Paul Duke**, with contributions from **Neeve McNabb** and other family members, the website includes:

- An interactive family tree.
- Individual family biographies and photographs.
- Historical research collected by Walter Craig Davidson.
- A searchable collection of 277 scanned research pages with accompanying OCR text.
- Family memories, poetry, and audio recordings.
- A downloadable, printable family-history book.

The surviving digitized research consists primarily of McNabb material, along with a smaller collection of McNeill records. Other portions of Craig's original research have not yet been located.

## The AI Experiment

In April 2025, the archive became the basis of an experimental AI-assisted genealogy project using xAI's Grok.

Within five days, 277 pages of research had been processed using Google Cloud Vision OCR, and a prototype was developed to explore the family tree and historical documents.

The experiment encountered limitations in AI interpretation of family relationships, but the digitization, search tools, and website remained useful.

The original prototype files are preserved in the repository as part of the project's history.

*Grok and Roll!*

## Research and Historical Accuracy

The archive combines historical documents, genealogical research, and family recollections.

Some information has not been independently verified. OCR text may also contain errors. When checking historical details, readers are encouraged to consult the original scanned documents.

The collection remains a work in progress as additional information and corrections become available.

## Contributions and Corrections

Family members with corrections, photographs, documents, or additional research are encouraged to contact **Neeve McNabb**.

We are particularly interested in locating other portions of Walter Craig Davidson's original genealogical research.
